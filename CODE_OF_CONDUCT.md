# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want **In to the Repo** to be a welcoming, harassment-free place for everyone who
opens an issue, sends a pull request, or joins a discussion, whatever their age, body
size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity
and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality,
personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that helps:

- Being kind, patient and respectful, especially to newcomers
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its users
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them

Examples of behavior that is not acceptable:

- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention of any kind
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing other people's private information, such as a physical or email address,
  without their explicit permission
- Plagiarism: passing off this project, or parts of it, as your own work
- Any other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces (this repository's issues, pull
requests, discussions and reviews) and when someone is officially representing the
project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

The project owner, Surakarapu Shanmukh Srinivas ([@shanmuckh](https://github.com/shanmuckh)),
is responsible for enforcing this Code of Conduct and may remove, edit, or reject
comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that do not follow it.

Report unacceptable behavior by contacting the owner privately through
[GitHub](https://github.com/shanmuckh). Every report will be reviewed promptly and
fairly, and the privacy of the reporter will be respected.

## Enforcement guidelines

1. **Correction** — for unprofessional or unwelcome behavior: a private written
   warning explaining what happened and why it was inappropriate.
2. **Warning** — for a single serious incident or repeated behavior: a warning with
   consequences, and no interaction with the people involved for a set period.
3. **Temporary ban** — for a serious violation: a temporary ban from all interaction
   with the project.
4. **Permanent ban** — for a pattern of violations, harassment, or aggression toward
   others: a permanent ban from all interaction with the project.
